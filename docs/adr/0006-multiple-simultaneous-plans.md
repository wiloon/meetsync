# A Gathering can resolve into more than one Plan

The matching step is not required to converge the group onto a single outcome. It's expected and allowed for a Gathering to produce several Plans at once — for example four people at dinner (with a Sponsor) while three others join an online game, and one person sits this Gathering out.

## Why

The group this was built for explicitly does not want to be forced together: some people said they're avoiding social events right now, others wanted very different activities. A tool that only ever outputs one winning (Topic, time) pair would implicitly pressure everyone toward one plan or force someone to be the odd one out with no path forward. Scoring per Topic (see [0002](0002-topic-centric-not-calendar-centric.md)) makes multiple simultaneous Plans a natural output rather than a special case.

## Consequences

- The UI must present "here are the plans that formed" rather than "here is the answer," which is a different framing than most scheduling tools default to.
- A Participant who matches no Plan at all is an expected, silent outcome, not an error state.
