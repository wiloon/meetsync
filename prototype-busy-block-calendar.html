Set your Busy Blocks

"General availability" is the Gathering-wide Busy Block (ADR-0003) — set once, applies to every topic. Each topic below layers a Topic Busy Block on top: hours already busy generally are locked, but you can mark extra time busy just for that one topic. Click a cell to toggle it, or click-and-drag across several to paint a range. 30-minute granularity — matches Rallly's own minimum poll duration, and the wider layout has room for it.
