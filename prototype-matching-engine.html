meetsync MatchingEngine — does the scoring feel right?

Question this demo answers: for a Topic (dinner, an online game, …), the engine looks at everyone who's interested, subtracts each person's Busy Blocks (both Gathering-wide and Topic-specific), and proposes the time window with the most weighted support. Do the results this produces match what a real group would expect, given the numbers picked in the spec: 30-minute slots, minimum 2 attendees, weight = 1 / priority rank, and windows within 10% of the top score shown as ties?
